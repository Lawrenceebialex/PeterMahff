Our Services

We provide essential dredging and marine support services tailored to the needs of construction government and private clients in Nigeria.

Channel Dredging

We deepen and maintain waterways ports and access channels so vessels can move safely and efficiently.

Land Reclamation

We create new usable land from water bodies for housing commercial development and infrastructure projects.

Sand Winning and Filling

We extract and supply quality sand for construction and fill low lying areas to the required levels.

Shore Protection

We protect coastlines riverbanks and waterfronts from erosion using proven dredging and placement methods.

River and Lake Dredging

We clear silt and deepen rivers and lakes to improve navigation reduce flooding and restore capacity.

Marine Support

We provide related logistics and support services that keep your marine and construction projects moving.
