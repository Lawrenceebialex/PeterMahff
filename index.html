What we do

Services

Core dredging and marine works for construction, infrastructure and waterway projects in the Niger Delta.

Channel dredging and maintenance

Deepening and maintaining access channels, ports and navigable waterways so vessels can move safely and efficiently.

Land reclamation and sand filling

Creating usable land from water bodies and filling low lying sites to the levels required for development.

Sand winning

Extracting and supplying quality sand for construction and civil works across the region.

Shoreline protection

Protecting riverbanks, coastlines and waterfronts from erosion through controlled dredging and placement.

Swamp and wetland excavation

Excavation and clearance works in swamp and wetland terrain typical of the Niger Delta environment.

Marine operational support

Coordination of barges, pipelines and supporting equipment for ongoing dredging and marine operations.

The company

P-MHAF Dredging

P-MHAF is a dredging and marine services company based in Warri, Delta State. We work on channel, reclamation and sand related projects across the riverine, swamp and coastal environments of the Niger Delta.

Our focus is practical delivery: clear scoping, careful mobilisation and steady communication with clients throughout the work. We serve construction contractors, private developers and organisations that need reliable local dredging capacity.
