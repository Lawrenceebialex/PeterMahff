Our Services

Professional dredging and marine support tailored for projects in Warri the Niger Delta and across Nigeria.

Channel Dredging

We deepen and maintain waterways ports and access channels so vessels can move safely and efficiently. Ideal for port approaches river mouths and industrial waterfronts.

Land Reclamation

We create new usable land from water bodies for housing commercial development and infrastructure projects. Careful placement and compaction for stable results.

Sand Winning and Filling

We extract and supply quality sand for construction and fill low lying areas to the required levels. Suitable for building sites roads and industrial platforms.

Shore Protection

We protect coastlines riverbanks and waterfronts from erosion using proven dredging and placement methods that respect local conditions.

River and Lake Dredging

We clear silt and deepen rivers and lakes to improve navigation reduce flooding risk and restore water holding capacity.

Marine Support

We provide related logistics and support services that keep your marine and construction projects moving smoothly from start to finish.
